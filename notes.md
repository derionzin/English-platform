
The idea is adding a system where xp can be gained, and with it levels can be gained. Then, with each level, the player will have the option of increasing one of its base stats:
Fire: increases damage efficiency calculations, giving a multiplier to all damage;
Water: increases max health and health calculations with a multiplier to all healing;
Earth: increases max defense and shield gain calculations;
Wind: increases delay calculations;
Night; increases max boost;
